  3. Identified why the sigreturn exploit doesn't crash

  The overflow at temp[256] (offset rbp-0x60) corrupts inner loop locals inside rleOpGeneric, NOT the return address (at rbp+0x8). This causes:
  - Infinite loop at 1024×1024 (corrupted loop state keeps iterating)
  - Normal completion at small render sizes (overflow doesn't reach critical variables)
  - The return address is 269 spans (2152 bytes) past temp[0] — need 270 total spans to corrupt RIP, but processing 270 masks at 1024×1024 is
  O(N²) and takes minutes
